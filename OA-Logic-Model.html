Goodwill Opportunity Accelerator® (OA) — Logic Model DRAFT

Two Connected Levels: How GII’s Network Work Enables Local Implementation
May 2026 Goodwill Industries International
How to read this page: This logic model shows how the Opportunity Accelerator works at two connected levels. Level 1 (Network) shows what GII does to build organizational capacity across the Goodwill network. Level 2 (Local) shows what member organizations do on the ground to serve participants. The bridge between them shows how GII’s work enables local implementation. Read each level left to right: Inputs → Activities → Outputs → Outcomes.
🌐 Level 1: Network (GII) What GII does to build organizational capacity
GII Inputs
  • OA shared practice model (25 service areas)
  • Adoption survey data (n=114)
  • MDRC evaluation partnership
  • CN certification (free to members)
  • OA Fellows program
  • TA capacity (GII mission team)
  • Fidelity tools & adoption criteria
  • MOLÉ & peer learning events
GII Activities
  • Develop & validate Theory of Action
  • Provide stage-differentiated TA
  • Deliver free CN certification
  • Facilitate OA Fellows program
  • Convene peer learning (MOLÉ, webinars, advisory councils)
  • Partner with MDRC for validation
  • Provide fidelity & self-assessment tools
GII Outputs
  • # orgs completing self-assessment
  • # receiving differentiated TA
  • # Career Navigators certified
  • # active OA Fellows
  • # peer learning sessions delivered
  • MDRC Phase 1 deliverables
  • Validated ToA documents
Organizational Outcomes
  • Orgs know where they are & what’s next
  • Staff understand roles (P / C / I)
  • Common language across network
  • GII support calibrated to stage
  • Orgs advance through maturity stages
  • Implementation more consistent
  • Collective impact story for funders
  • Peer learning with common measures
GII’s work produces organizations that are clear, consistent and equipped ▶ which enables local implementation
🏢 Level 2: Local (Member Organizations) What organizations do on the ground
Org Inputs
  • Committed Mission Leader
  • Aligned staffing (8 functional roles)
  • Certified Career Navigators
  • Employer partnerships
  • Community connections
  • Data systems & ART
  • Funding & facilities
Org Activities
  • Outreach & recruitment
  • Registration & intake
  • Administer valid assessments
  • Co-create ICFPs
  • Coordinate wraparound supports
  • Deliver skills training (foundational, digital, occ-specific)
  • Career exploration & job search prep
  • Place in employment
  • Retention follow-up (30–365 days)
  • Alumni engagement
Org Outputs
  • # reached through outreach
  • # completing intake
  • # assessed with valid instruments
  • # with individualized career plan
  • # receiving wraparound supports
  • # completing training programs
  • # placed in employment
  • # with retention follow-up
  • # engaged as alumni
Participant Outcomes
  • Services matched to assessed needs
  • Clear plan with goals & action steps
  • Barriers identified & addressed
  • Job-relevant skills built
  • Placed in quality employment
  • Employment retained & advanced
  • Financial literacy & stability growing
  • Ongoing connection through alumni

★ Long-Term Impact

Enhanced employment outcomes Career advancement & economic mobility Financial stability Strengthened organizational capacity Network cohesion Community economic strength

📋 Assumptions

  1. Five maturity stages represent meaningful differences in practice
  2. Role clarity will improve implementation
  3. Mission Leaders will invest in org preparation if they see a clear structure
  4. A progressive roadmap will motivate action
  5. Organizations have or can obtain the resources to progress
  6. GII can provide differentiated support at scale
  7. Participants will engage with the full continuum of services if offered
  8. Stakeholders will engage authentically in the validation process

⚠️ External Factors

  • Labor market conditions (regional variation)
  • Funding environment (federal, state, private)
  • Policy & regulatory landscape
  • Community demographics & population needs
  • Economic conditions (recession, inflation, housing)
