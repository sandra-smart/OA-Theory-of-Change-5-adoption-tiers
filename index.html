The Challenge

Quality depends too much on which door someone walks through. The OA was designed to change that.

Unequal Access

Organizations with the most resources provide the most. Communities with the greatest need often have the fewest tools. There is no standard of services across the network.

🚧

The Real Barrier

It's not resistance — members believe in the OA. The barrier is capacity, systems and change management. Staff wear too many hats. Lack of clarity on who owns what.

Adoption Tiers

Five stages. Data-driven.

Derived from the March 2026 survey
AProspective
(Non-adopters)Limited mission programming and has not expressed interest in OA
BExploring
(Partial adopters)Has started to implement aspects of OA into mission programs
CProgressing
(Partial adopters)Partial OA adopter with key gaps and concerns to be addressed
DReady
(Gold)Is close to or on par with OA Gold criteria
EExemplary
(Platinum)Is close to or on par with OA Platinum; true champions of OA

Why five instead of four? 54% of the network fell into one "Partial Adopter" category — hiding two very different populations. Splitting them unlocks targeted support.

If → Then → Then → Outcomes

→
📚
Phase 2: Equip & Connect
Deliver the Right Services
Training (foundational, digital and occupational), wraparound support, financial education, career exploration, job search and interview prep

Launch Careers, Stay Connected

LEADING TO: Enhanced Employment · Career Advancement · Economic Mobility · Financial Stability

This logic works in reverse — if outcomes break down, trace back to find where. MDRC will help validate which links carry the most weight.

How Organizations Connect to Support

Three steps from first contact to the right level of help.

📧

Step 1: Pre-Engagement

Email outreach with OA success stories and real data. Discovery webinars. OA Fellows invitations. CEO and mission leader engagement.

📊

Step 2: Discovery Call

Review an adoption dashboard built from survey data. Understand current programming. Identify areas meeting OA requirements and key gaps. Prioritize and create an initial action plan.

🤝

Step 3: Match to Support

Based on the discovery call, connect the organization to the right level of GII support — self-directed, group advisory or individualized consultation.
